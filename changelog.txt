Version: 0.6.0
Date: 2026-10-26
  Features:
    - Renamed the mod and every internal identifier from chipotrio to chiptorio.
    - Removed enemies entirely: enemy autoplace is stripped from the map generator, enemy expansion and evolution are disabled, and existing enemy entities are destroyed on init.
    - Removed robots and the spidertron: roboports, logistic and construction robots, robot equipment, logistic request chests and their technologies are hidden and unbuildable.
    - Removed combat progression: weapons, ammo, capsules, turrets, walls, gates, mines, tanks and artillery are hidden, their recipes are disabled, and the remaining technology graph is rewired around them.
    - Added a data-contract and runtime test probe plus tools/test-all.ps1.
  Balance:
    - Removed the sustained-production throughput gate and its strict-compute setting. Research requirements will move to network compute, memory and bandwidth in the next milestone.
    - Removed microprocessor and system-on-chip costs from roboports and GPU and quantum-processor costs from the spidertron; those prototypes are no longer buildable.
  Bugfixes:
    - The data stage no longer requires the deleted technology-gate metadata module.

Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026-10-26
  Features:
    - Added The Answer endgame: a 5x5 Answer Engine assembled from every era of the factory.
    - Added The Question, an infinite final technology that costs all seven workloads plus microprocessors and requires every Chiptorio technology.
    - Booting the Answer Engine requires sustained production of all seven workloads and microprocessors. Dropping below any target pauses the 42-minute clock until every line recovers.
    - Added a custom Answer Engine panel showing every required rate, the remaining time, the current question level and the answer score.
    - Completing the engine prints 42, plays a generated chiptune fanfare, sets a custom victory screen, and unlocks post-game score scaling.
    - Generated the Answer Engine icon, The Question technology icon, updated thumbnail and victory image.
  Balance:
    - The final exam requires 10/s punched cards, 10/s punched tape, 10/s magnetic tape, 6/s disk packs, 4/s solid-state drives, 2/s mainframe modules, 0.5/s qubit arrays and 1/s microprocessors.
    - Every level of The Question raises the engine's demand by 25% and the completion score by 42 points.
  Bugfixes:
    - None.

Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-10-26
  Features:
    - Added Era 7: superconductors, cryogenics, physical qubits, error-corrected qubits and quantum processors.
    - Added the Quantum Fab (fluid-capable, chiptorio-quantum) and the Cryostat, a fifth research computer with eight module slots and 4x research speed.
    - Added a seventh workload, the Qubit Array, produced in the Quantum Fab and consumed by every research computer.
    - Added cryogen, produced in the Cooling Tower from coolant and water.
    - Added three technologies: Quantum Computing, Quantum Error Correction and Quantum Advantage.
    - All three Era 7 gates measure sustained production: multi-core CPUs, physical qubits and error-corrected qubits.
    - Added a quantum processor ingredient to the spidertron.
    - Generated icons for every new item, fluid, machine and technology.
  Balance:
    - Quantum Error Correction costs Qubit Arrays but its recipe deliberately needs only physical qubits, so the tech is not circular.
    - Quantum Advantage requires a running error-correction line; a stockpile of logical qubits cannot satisfy the gate.
  Bugfixes:
    - None.

Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-10-26
  Features:
    - Added Era 5-6: VLSI fabrication, the datacenter cooling loop, chiplets, HBM stacks, GPUs and multi-core CPUs.
    - Added the VLSI Fab (fluid-capable, chiptorio-vlsi) and the Cooling Tower (chiptorio-cooling).
    - Added the Datacenter, a fourth research computer with six module slots and 2.5x research speed.
    - Added phase-shift masks, CMP slurry, low-k dielectric, coolant and cooling units.
    - Added four technologies: VLSI Process, Datacenter, Advanced Packaging and Heterogeneous Compute.
    - Advanced Packaging and Heterogeneous Compute gate on sustained SoC and GPU production, not science packs.
    - Generated icons for every new item, fluid, machine and technology.
  Balance:
    - The Tube Mainframe and Mainframe Cluster recipes are now correctly locked behind their technologies instead of being craftable from the start.
    - Assembling machine 3 now needs a microprocessor; beacons and roboports need a system on a chip; spidertrons need GPUs.
    - VLSI gates are tuned around the existing utility-science (mainframe module) line.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed a fresh-save softlock: hiding the vanilla lab removed the trigger that unlocks the first science pack. The automation-science-pack trigger now fires when the first Analytical Engine is crafted.
    - Fixed the Chiptorio item-group icon rendering at the wrong size (128px vs the generated 64px icon).
    - Fixed the throughput gate applying 2% decay per second instead of per minute. Research is now throttled proportionally to production, with proportional decay reaching the documented 2%/min at zero production.
