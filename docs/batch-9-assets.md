# Batch 9 — first item-icon refresh

Candidate authoring, 2026-11-09. Five individual 64×64 transparent icons are
rendered from native Blender geometry through Asset Studio, using HIP GPU
Cycles at 64 samples. They retain their existing item names and dimensions.

- 3D NAND Module: green edge-connector module with three stacked-die packages.
- Accelerator Board: GPU thermal assembly, four HBM packages, power capacitors
  and an expansion bracket on a green PCB.
- Advanced Control Board: red PCB, flat controller package, regulator bank and
  plated component leads, distinct from the accelerator silhouette.
- AGI Model: a stored-model cartridge with gold contacts and a teal graph inset.
- Analytical Engine: miniature brass register columns, open frame, visible drive
  gear and crank on a mechanical bed. No broad roof hides the mechanism.

Exact output PNGs are inspected at 64×64 and 32×32, plus enlarged previews.
Each icon is a separate final-art candidate in the one-at-a-time review queue.
They also retain downloadable Blender sources. Batch bundles stay in production
history; no automatic approval, installation or publication occurs. Existing
generated icons are untouched until exact-art approval, isolated validation and
explicit publication.
