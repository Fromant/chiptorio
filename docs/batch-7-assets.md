# Batch 7 — cooling, quantum fabrication and network infrastructure

Candidate authoring, 2026-11-08. The next-batch request uses the square sizes
recorded in the machine art-direction plan: Cooling Tower 7×7, Quantum Fab
11×11, Switch and Router 3×3, Memory Bank 5×5. Installed footprints, recipes,
capacities, networking and power remain unchanged pending publication.

- Cooling Tower: twin fin-and-tube radiator cores, axial fans, vertical headers,
  controlled pipe elbows and saddles. Four native directions rotate the machine
  and its front input/back output ports together. Native Factorio assembler pipe
  pictures and covers finish the connectors in game; Blender shows their seats.
- Quantum Fab: a stepped dilution refrigerator, cold-plate feed braids, an
  indexed substrate line, RF probe scan and a carrier test indicator. Insulated
  supply and return lines follow separate service lanes with bounded elbows,
  without the earlier sweeping exterior curves. Loading,
  alignment, field work and unloading are marked on the Blender timeline.
- Network Switch: four-sided Ethernet port banks around an exposed switch ASIC,
  with visible top and front link lamps.
- Network Router: a taller routing spine, uplink banks and redundant antennas,
  with blue link heartbeats.
- Memory Bank: three dense blade banks, edge connectors, capacity bus and top
  readiness lenses. The previously accepted geometry is retained.

Native crafting owns the cooling and quantum loops (192 source frames at 24 fps;
96 final frames at 12 fps). The three container devices have explicit scripted
owners (48 source / 24 final frames at 12 fps). Switch/router heartbeats indicate
an actual graph link, not packet volume. Memory heartbeats require both a graph
link and contributed memory capacity; empty/disconnected banks remain idle.
These overlays are enabled only when the corresponding artwork is published.
Fluid bindings preserve every original connection and its direction on the new
perimeter. All models are staged through Asset Studio and receive GPU-rendered
seven-camera inspections before review. No shipping files are installed here.

Inspection: Cycles HIP on the Radeon RX 9070 XT, CPU render devices disabled.
Game, front, left, right, rear, top and detail pose sheets were visually checked.
The Quantum Fab probe tip was raised to clear the substrate; the whole-cycle
moving-part scan reports no intersections. Cooling fan blades clear their guards;
the two reported spindle/hub contacts are intentional mounted joints. All three
electronic models were checked at lit and unlit phases. Model build checks pass for loop
closure, floor/footprint envelope and zero idle emission. Exact `.blend` files and
diagnostic sheets are available in the one-at-a-time Model and motion queue.
The infrastructure smoke test uses a disposable mod copy and synthetic sprite
fixtures to test directional animation definitions, native connector pictures,
link/memory transitions and all 16 fluid connections across four orientations.
It does not approve or publish production artwork.

The web library displays exact approved artwork even before publication, with
an explicit **Approved artwork · not published** label. Installed snapshots are
retained separately for candidate comparisons; library display is not publication.
