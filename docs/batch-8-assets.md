# Batch 8 — Datacenter compute-provider hall

Candidate authoring, 2026-11-08. This batch contains the Datacenter provider,
distinct from the Datacenter Lab and the Training Cluster. Its planned square
11×11 footprint contains eight compute racks and four memory cabinets, three
service walkways, overhead fabric/power trays, front distribution cabinets and
a rear cooling plant. The rack counts communicate the provider's existing
eight computer/four memory slots; decorative sleds are not extra capacity.

The rear plant has fin-and-tube exchangers, four guarded fans and short, supported
manifold connections. Separate supply/return lanes run beside the rack columns.
Bounded pipe elbows avoid sweeping curves through equipment. Low front service
islands and open rack aisles preserve the fixed game-camera view into the hall.

Working art uses 96 source frames at 24 fps / 48 final frames at 12 fps. Green
hardware links and staggered amber online bursts are gated by actual network
connectivity and contributed compute or memory capacity. They do not measure
packet throughput or CPU utilization. Empty, irrelevant-cargo-only and
disconnected Datacenters remain idle; idle emission is off. Runtime art stays
guarded until the corresponding animation is published.

Model review is staged through Asset Studio with a downloadable `.blend`,
timeline markers and seven-camera GPU inspection sheets. Whole-cycle checks
cover geometry closure, footprint bounds, floor envelope and idle emission.
The contact report distinguishes mechanical leads for visual inspection from
an automatic collision verdict. Publication and final sprite approval remain
separate from this model stage. Installed bounds, power, cooling, capacities,
recipes and earlier approved artwork are unchanged by staging this candidate.

Inspection uses Cycles HIP on the Radeon RX 9070 XT with CPU render devices
disabled. Front, rear, both sides, top, game and close-up views were checked.
The whole-cycle moving-part scan reports four spindle/hub joints; fan blades
clear the casing and close grilles. A duplicated cross-hall cable-tray mesh was
removed during inspection before sealing the review revision.

The infrastructure smoke test overlays synthetic sprites in a disposable mod
copy and verifies the 11×11 prototype binding, 48-frame runtime animation,
computer-only/memory-only activation, hardware removal, irrelevant inventory and
disconnection. Production files are not modified by that test.
