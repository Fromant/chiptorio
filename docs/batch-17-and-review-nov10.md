# Batch 17 and November 10 review revisions

Five new 64×64 RGBA candidates: HBM Module, HBM Stack Workload, HBM Stack,
HBM3 Module and Inference Lab. HIP GPU Cycles uses 64 samples. Stack is a single
tall bonded-die package; Module has two stacks on an interposer; HBM3 has four
stacks and a controller. Workload uses a violet test carrier with two stacks and
a readout plaque. These compositions distinguish icons, not gameplay capacities.
Inference Lab reuses its existing entity authoring source.

Three rejected icons are revised separately:

- Transistor: a larger neutral silver heat-spreader tab with a screw through-hole,
  black resin body and nickel leads. No copper/gold colour on the mounting tab.
- GPU: dual fans retained, with silver edge rails, angular shroud braces,
  perforations, fasteners, exposed fin/heatpipe details, auxiliary power socket
  and unequal PCIe finger groups separated by a real key gap.
- Computer Assembler: a newly composed, coherent assembly station with solid
  base, two rail columns, carriage, insertion head and open computer chassis.
  The entity model is not altered by this icon-only revision.

The user-supplied SketchUp link was inaccessible through the browsing tool.
Visual reference: [Sapphire NITRO cooler accessories](https://www.sapphiretech.co/en/accessories/nitro-gear-accessories).
Geometry is authored locally; no external model is redistributed. Colours use
new material values rather than sampling the installed generated icons.

Exact PNGs are checked at 64px and 32px, then staged individually for review.
Previously accepted icons and world models are not rebuilt or unlocked.
No publication or prototype changes occur during candidate production.
